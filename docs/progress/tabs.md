# Design tabs (several designs open at once)

Ben, 2026-11-12: "the ability to have multiple tabs open at once". Like Fusion's document tabs (behaviour copied, never names, icons or looks).

What already exists (2026-11-06, Design tabs 1 to 3, PRs #416 and #434): a strip under the top bar, one tab per open design with its name and a chip for the Lab it was showing (DESIGNLAB, SLICELAB), + opens a new design, × closes (asking first when the design on screen has unsaved changes), the tabs come back after a reload, and each tab keeps its own undo steps, selection and camera while it is not on screen. On a phone (640 px and narrower) the strip is one switcher button with + beside it and a short list. A tab is a kept project: switching keeps the one being left (its own autosave) and opens the other, so only one design is loaded in the kernel at a time. Code: apps/fission/src/design-tabs.ts (shell side), packages/ui/src/design-tabs.ts (strip and list rules), styles/design-tabs.css; checks e2e/tabs.mjs, packages/ui/test/design-tabs.test.ts.

It was built behind the home screen's switch, which is still off, and its browser check ran only by hand, so by 2026-11-12 it failed (only because of the Lab names: the chip now reads DESIGNLAB).
