# Laser workspace (L…)

The laser cutter built into SpindleLabs (Ben, 2026-11-11: "a built in laser cutter"). Behind `?laser` until Ben releases it. No kernel files: shapes come from Design through the representation registry, 2D curve maths stays in the kernel (offsets for kerf come with K1, step 52).

Ben has no laser of his own; he uses his school's ("a big red one"). Until its make and controller are known, the files (SVG, DXF) are the way to the machine, and the machine output stays generic.

## Steps

- [x] L1a. The Laser page: Design's sketches and profiles listed (Design's `design:outlines` lister), laid out in rows on the bed (size, gap, sheet thickness kept in the project), drawn from above with the kernel's own extrude, saved as SVG and DXF at true size with exact arcs. Verified: hand-worked unit tests (layout, moves, the SVG and DXF text, bulge decimals), deliberate breaks each caught (bulge sign, open polyline, right-edge gap, spacing, a stale view drawn, bulge rounding, a suppressed sketch listed), the browser check `apps/fission/e2e/laser-page.mjs`, and an outside reader: ezdxf 1.4.5 reads the DXF as R12 in mm with no audit errors, and the areas from its own arcs match the hand-worked ones (slot 800 + 100π = 1114.159265359, quarter-arc triangle 2 + π/2 − 1 = 2.570796327, to 9 decimals); svgelements reads the SVG's paths closed at the right places.
- [x] L1a2. The flat view (Ben, 2026-11-12: "laser cutting is 2D"): the bed drawn straight from above as an outlined sheet at true scale with a ruler, each part as its outline with its name, sliding and zooming only (no orbit, no view cube, no Top/Front/Right; its own Zoom out, Zoom in and Fit in the bottom bar's look), over the shared viewport's empty 2D view so Design's camera comes back untouched. Drawn from the saved file's own path data, so the kernel is asked nothing and the screen can't differ from the file. The sheet-thickness box is gone until something uses it (the value a project holds is kept). Verified: hand-worked unit tests of the camera (fit, zoom about a point, limits, the ruler's steps, margins clear of the parts list; 0.9× and 1.1× at each limit), the view in happy-dom (what is drawn where, wheel, drag, pinch, keys), 22 deliberate breaks each caught, and the browser check at 1280 and 375 px (one true scale for bed and part, nothing 3D left, the wheel about the pointer, Design's camera the same after).
- [ ] L1b. Parts placed by hand: a part dragged on the bed and turned (quarter turns, a typed angle), its place kept in the project by its id in Design (`LaserData.placed`; parts without one are still laid out in rows, round the placed ones), Arrange putting all back in rows. Warnings in words and on the part: off the bed, and overlapping another part (exactly, asked of the kernel; boxes first). Starts from: `flat-view.ts` (pointer handling: a press on a `path.laser-flat-part` moves the part, elsewhere slides the view), `sheet.ts` `layOut` and `moveOutline`, `page.ts` `LaserData`.
- [ ] L1c. Layers: cut, engrave and score, a colour per layer (as laser programs map colours to layers) in the SVG and a DXF layer each; a part's layer chosen in its row; drawn in its layer's colour. Speed, power and passes wait for L3 (the files can't carry them).
- [ ] L1d. Kerf: the beam's width kept with the bed, a warning where two cuts are closer than it; then each cut moved out by half of it (holes in) from the kernel's 2D offsets (K1 with step 52), refused in words where a gap is narrower than the kerf.
- [ ] L1e. Flat faces of bodies as parts (a Design producer for a planar face's outline).
- [ ] L3a. The beam's path in order (inner loops before outer, nearest next), shown as a preview, with the job's time from speeds and travel.
- [ ] L3b. G-code for GRBL lasers (M4 dynamic power, S from power and the machine's maximum), behind a machine profile; other controllers once Ben's school machine is known.
- [ ] L4a. Sending to the machine over Web Serial, only if it can be made safe: never starts by itself, a start the person presses, a stop always on screen, the machine's own interlocks never bypassed.

Later ideas, not planned yet: nesting parts to save sheet, finger-jointed boxes from a solid (X13), engraving pictures from Image, sheet-metal flat patterns (step 87).
