Privacy and terms

Last changed 5 November 2026. Every change to this page is public in the project's history.

Privacy

The short version: SpindleLabs has no ads, no analytics, no cookies and no trackers. Without an account, your designs stay in your browser on your device: the people who make SpindleLabs never receive them. An account is optional. Everything works without one. If you make one, we keep your name, email address, plan and the choices listed below, and the designs you keep in your account (My designs), which you can turn off.

Who we are

SpindleLabs is made by Ben, a student in Perth, Western Australia, and run by his father, Christopher Pyvis, who is responsible for the personal information described here. Write to ben@spindlelabs.app about anything on this page.

What is kept on your device

  • Your projects, and the copy kept as you work, are saved in this browser's own storage on this device. Without an account they are not sent anywhere. If you sign in and keep designs in your account, they are also sent there (see My designs below). Use Save to keep a copy as a file.
  • Settings, including the AI assistant's settings and any AI key you enter, are kept in this browser's storage on this device.
  • If you sign in, the sign-in itself (a token that proves it is you, with your name and email address) and your last answer to the email question (so your account shows it straight away) are kept in this browser's storage until you sign out.
  • If you keep designs in your account, this browser also keeps a list of which project here is which design in your account (with a fingerprint of each one's contents, so only changed projects are sent again) and the workspace each was last saved from. It stays when you sign out, so signing in again doesn't send everything twice, and goes when you delete your account here or clear the site's data.
  • If you download the in-browser AI model, its files (about 0.9 GB) are kept in this browser's cache.

Your account, if you make one

You can sign in with your Google account. You never have to: designing, slicing and saving work the same without an account.

  • What we keep: your Google account's name and email address, an ID for your account, when you made it, whether you said yes to emails and when, your plan (free, Pro or Team) and when it renews or ends, if you pay for Pro the IDs Stripe gives your customer record and subscription and its status (see Payments), whether keeping designs in your account is on and, on a plan that includes AI, how much of its AI allowance you have used. If you keep designs in your account, we also keep those designs, as described under My designs below.
  • What sign-in keeps for security: what Google sends when you sign in (your name, email address, Google's ID for you and a link to your Google profile picture), when you last signed in, and the IP address and browser of each signed-in session, until you sign out or delete your account. The service's own records of sign-ins are deleted within 30 days.
  • What we don't keep: designs you don't keep in your account, your picture itself, your Google password, your contacts or anything else in your Google account. You see what Google will share on Google's own page before you agree.
  • Why: to sign you in, to keep your plan and your designs the same on every device you sign in on, and to email you only if you said yes.
  • Where: your account is kept by Supabase, on servers in Sydney, Australia, under Supabase's privacy policy. Supabase is a company based in the United States; it stores your account in Sydney and handles it only to run the service for us. Signing in goes through Google, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Who sees it: only the people who run SpindleLabs, to look after your account. We never sell it, and never share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

My designs

When you're signed in, SpindleLabs keeps your designs in your account, so you can open them on any device you sign in on. This is on unless you turn it off.

  • What is kept: for each design, its project file (the same as Save would write, including anything you put in it, such as pictures or notes), its name, a small picture of it taken from the 3D view, which workspace it was last saved from (Design, Slice and so on), when it was saved and when it reached your account.
  • When it is sent: a few seconds after a project in this browser is saved, and only if it changed. The first time you sign in on a browser that already has projects, nothing is sent until you choose whether to keep them in your account, because on a shared computer they may be someone else's. If you choose Not now, they stay only in the browser until you choose otherwise in My designs.
  • Earlier versions: when a newer save replaces a design in your account, the account keeps the version it replaces, so you can go back to it from any device (File, Earlier versions…). It keeps at most one every 10 minutes and the newest 20 of each design. Nothing extra is sent for this: the account keeps the copy it already has.
  • Who sees it: only you, on the devices you sign in on. Nobody else can see your designs through SpindleLabs. The people who run SpindleLabs can reach the account service's database to look after it, but don't look at your designs unless you ask us to help with one, or the law requires it.
  • Limits: up to 200 designs and 20 MB in all, each design up to 5 MB, with earlier versions counted in the 20 MB. When a save would go over, the oldest earlier versions are deleted first to make room; a design that is still too big, or that doesn't fit, stays only in your browser, and SpindleLabs says so.
  • Turning it off: untick Keep my designs in my account in My designs (File, My designs…). Nothing more is sent from any of your devices; the designs already kept stay until you delete them.
  • Deleting: Delete in My designs deletes a design and its earlier versions from your account straight away (the copy in your browser stays). Deleting a project from your browser doesn't delete it from your account. Deleting your account deletes every design in it. Copies in our service's backups are gone within 30 days.

Emails

We email you about new features and SpindleLabs Pro only if you tick the box that asks, which always starts unticked. You can untick it in your account at any time, and every email we send has a link to stop them. Emails about your account itself (for example, that it was deleted) don't need the box.

Payments

SpindleLabs Pro is paid for through Stripe, a payment company. For now Pro is being tested: payments are in Stripe's test mode, which takes no real money, and Pro's features are not built yet. This page will say so before real payments start.

  • What Stripe keeps: when you choose Upgrade, you pay on Stripe's own page. Stripe keeps your card details, your email address, your billing country and the record of each payment, under Stripe's privacy policy, and checks payments for fraud. Your card number never reaches SpindleLabs.
  • What we keep: the ID of your Stripe customer record and of your subscription, and its status (for example active or cancelled), so your account shows the right plan. We are told by Stripe when you start, change or cancel Pro.
  • What we send Stripe: your account's email address and ID, so the payment is linked to your account.
  • Cancelling: open your account and choose Manage or cancel Pro, which opens Stripe's own page. Pro then stays until the end of the time you paid for and does not renew. Deleting your account cancels Pro at Stripe first, so nothing more is charged.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the site. SpindleLabs is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address and browser details when you load it, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Signing in, if you choose to: your browser talks to Google and to Supabase as described above, and sends the designs you keep in your account to Supabase. With no account, neither is contacted.
  • Paying for Pro, if you choose to: your browser goes to Stripe's page to pay or to manage Pro, and our account service talks to Stripe as described under Payments. Without choosing Upgrade, Stripe is never contacted.
  • The AI assistant. Nothing is sent to an online AI service unless you choose one and give it your own key. When you open the assistant, an AI that runs on your own device may be chosen for you; that one keeps everything on your device.
    • With Anthropic, OpenAI or another address you type, your request and a short summary of your project (the names and sizes of its features and any recent errors, not the shapes themselves) go straight from your browser to that provider, using your own key. Before the first request to each address, SpindleLabs shows exactly what will be sent and waits for you. That provider's own privacy policy and terms then apply, and you pay them directly. SpindleLabs never sees your requests or your key.
    • With Ollama on your own computer, nothing leaves your computer. If you point it at another computer's address, it is treated like any other provider and asks you first.
    • The in-browser model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and GitHub, and after that runs on your device: your requests stay there.
  • Printing. Print files are made in your browser and saved to your device. SpindleLabs doesn't connect to your printer or send anything about it anywhere.
  • Files you save or export (projects, print files, STL files and the like) are saved to your device: you choose the name and, in browsers that offer it, the folder. Where they go next is up to you.

Children and students

To make an account you need to be old enough to have your own Google account (13 in Australia; older in some countries). Without an account SpindleLabs asks for no personal information, so anyone can use it. If you are under 18, ask a parent before paying for anything. The hosted AI providers set their own minimum ages (many require you to be 18, some allow 13 with a parent's permission). If you are under a provider's age, don't use a key for it; Ollama and the in-browser model need no account.

Seeing, changing and deleting your data

  • Your account: sign in and open your account. Download my data gives you everything we keep about you as a file, including every design in your account and its earlier versions. Delete my account deletes your account and everything kept with it straight away. Copies in our service's backups are gone within 30 days. Stripe keeps records of payments for 5 years, because Australian tax law requires payment records to be kept; deleting your account deletes our link to them. You can also email us to see, correct or delete anything.
  • On your device: to remove an AI key, open the assistant panel and choose Remove key. To delete everything kept in this browser (projects, settings, keys, the sign-in and any downloaded model), clear the site data for spindlelabs.app in your browser's settings. This can't be undone, so save any project you want to keep to a file first.
  • For anything you sent to an AI provider, ask that provider.
  • If you think we have mishandled your information, tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, or to the privacy regulator where you live.
