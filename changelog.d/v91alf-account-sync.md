fix

- On a shared computer, projects made while you were signed out are no longer sent to your account at your next sign-in without asking: My designs asks first, as on your first sign-in (issue #628 item 5).
- Settings changed while signed out (printer fit, units, theme) are kept and sent at your next sign-in on the same browser, instead of being replaced by the account's older ones (issue #628 item 6).
- A big design on a slow upload is given the time it needs, and one that still runs out of time no longer stops your other designs from being kept (issue #628 item 7).
- Opening a design last saved on a device whose clock was ahead no longer stops this browser keeping earlier versions of it (issue #628 item 8).
- A project whose design was deleted from your account on another device, then changed here, can be kept in your account again from My designs (issue #628 item 2).
