- Settings that follow you: when you're signed in, your theme and its colours, units and Slice for printing choices (your slicer presets, the preset picked last, the nozzle, the warm-up loop and Better grip) are kept in your account and are the same on every device you sign in on. Changes made on two devices are put together setting by setting and preset by preset, and a new device shows your theme straight away, without reloading.
