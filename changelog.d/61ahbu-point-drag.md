- With Select, a sketch point can be dragged and the rest of the sketch follows its constraints: a rectangle kept square stays square, a point held level with a fixed one slides along. Fixed points and points that follow a body say why they can't be dragged, and one drag is one undo step.
- A sketch with constraints stays solved when the body it is attached to changes: an arc pinned to a corner keeps its ends on one circle, and a sketch whose constraints can't hold there says so.
