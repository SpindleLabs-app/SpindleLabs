fix

- Settings that follow you: turning Keep my settings off and on again on one device no longer removes another device's own settings (slicer presets, printer fit measurements) when it next catches up.
- On a shared computer, the settings someone else left in the browser are no longer put into the next person's account when they sign in; they stay only in that browser.
- A change made while settings were being brought from another device no longer undoes that other device's change to the theme, units or another slicer preset.
- An older SpindleLabs tab left open no longer wipes Settings entries or slicer preset choices made by a newer version.
- A setting changed in the few seconds before signing out, or while offline, is sent the next time you sign in instead of being lost; signing out mid-sync leaves nothing behind.
- The privacy page says what this browser keeps for this.
