- Sketches have a Dimension tool: click a line to set its length, an arc for its radius, a circle for its diameter, or two points for the distance between them, then type the size and press Enter. The sketch moves to fit, and the size is shown on the sketch.
- Double-click a size (or tap it with the Dimension tool) to change it. A size that fights the others, or that is already set by them, is refused with the reason, and the sizes that fight are shown in red.
- Sketches have construction lines: pick lines, circles or arcs with Select and press X (or the Construction button) to turn them into dashed helper lines that guide the drawing but are left out of the shape. Press X again to turn them back. Constraints and sizes still hold them.
- Projects saved with construction lines or sizes use a newer design file version, which older copies of the app will say is from a newer SpindleLabs.
