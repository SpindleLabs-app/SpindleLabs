# Design workspace

Contents of `workspaces/design`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- the CAD workspace (`workspace.ts`, which names each undo step: "Change width" merged per size box, "Change shape to …"; a mount by Undo or Redo keeps the camera and keeps whatever in the selection still resolves by name on the bodies it rebuilt (`lastScene`); `profiles.ts` ready-made outlines until the sketcher).
- `commands.ts`: Design's commands as data (`DESIGN_COMMANDS`: `set_part`, `create_sketch` (AI7a: an empty sketch on an origin plane; `documentFor` refuses a face, which only `run` can place), `draw_circle`, `draw_rectangle`, `draw_line` (AI7b: in sketch mm within `SKETCH_REACH`, drawn by `drawingFor` into the sketch just started, or by `run` into the one open; `drawRefused` refuses a sketch an extrude is built from; `drawWords` names each), `extrude_sketch` (AI7c: `EXTRUDE_OP`, a signed distance, negative the other way and recorded as Flip; `extrudeTarget` picks the sketch named, else the open one, else the newest before the marker (`extrudable`); `documentFor` records it with `extrudeSketch`, and Design's `run` does what the Extrude tool's OK does, `check` having had the kernel build it first), `rename_item` (AI7d: `RENAME_OP`, a `name` and an optional `item`; `renameTarget` takes the item named, else the one just before the marker, i.e. what the answer just made; `documentFor` renames it with `renameItem` (timeline-edits.ts), refusing the timeline's own causes; `renameWords` names it), `export_stl`), used by the toolbar and the AI assistant alike; `set_part`'s depth and height are optional (a left-out size keeps the current one in `run`; `documentFor` refuses one the shape needs), tested in `test/commands.test.ts`.
- `ai-view.ts` (step 25c3): `designAIView`, what the AI assistant is told (the mounted workspace's `aiView`): the part as the set_part that makes it, the panel's sizes a set_part keeps, the timeline in build order with each item's state (the last `AI_TIMELINE_ITEMS`), the marker, and whether Accept changes the part or adds a body (`adds`: where `withPart` puts it). Hand-worked in `test/ai-view.test.ts`; seeded timelines against the build model and `documentFor` in packages/testing `test/ai-view.test.ts`. AI7a: also the commands the AI may use (`aiCommands`: set_part and create_sketch, its `on` the origin planes plus the flat faces on screen from `aiFaces`, at most `AI_FACES`), words for each place (`choiceWords`, `facingWords`), and the prompt's lines on the sketch create_sketch adds and the faces it can go on. AI7b: the draw commands, the open sketch (or that none is), what x and y mean on each plane (`SKETCH_AXES`) and each face's centre in its own sketch (`AIFace.inSketch`). AI7c: `extrude_sketch`, its `sketch` choice (the last `AI_SKETCHES` sketches by name, only when there are some) and the lines saying which sketch a bare extrude takes. AI7d: `rename_item`, its `item` choice (the last `AI_TIMELINE_ITEMS` items by name, only when there are any) and the line on what a rename changes.
