# The Laser workspace

Contents of `workspaces/laser`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. The steps are in `docs/progress/laser.md`. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- `src/index.ts`: the plugin. With `?laser` (flag.ts) it mounts the page; without it, the placeholder it was.
- `src/flag.ts`: the `?laser` switch (as Design's `?combine`: kept for the tab, `?laser=off` clears it), off until Ben releases it.
- `src/page.ts`: the Laser page (L1a, L1a2, L1b1). Lists Design's flat shapes through the registry (`objectsOf("design:outlines")`, Design's representations.ts `DESIGN_OUTLINES`) and delivers each as an exact outline; never reads Design's code or copies its shapes into the project. The project keeps only `LaserData` (bed, gap, parts turned off, `placed`: where parts were put by hand, by their id in Design; a sheet thickness is kept but has no box until something uses it). The view is flat (flat-view.ts) over the shared viewport's empty 2D view (`viewport.showImage(null)`, given back with `clearImage()` on sleep and unmount, so Design's body and camera return as they were); the kernel is asked nothing to draw. Parts start in rows; the first one moved or turned (a drag, the arrows, Turn, or X, Y and Angle typed under the picked part's name) fixes every part on the bed where it is, so nothing else shifts; "Back in the rows" frees one, "Arrange in rows" all. A part past the bed's edge is said in words and marked, never moved back, and the files still save. Pairs whose boxes overlap or all but touch (`overlapCandidates` with overlap.ts's `BOX_MARGIN`) are asked of the kernel through `partsRelation`, each answer kept for the two outlines as they sit so a redraw asks nothing again: an overlap (and too close to tell) is said and marked on both parts, a touch only said. The picked part is the page's own for now (the shared selection comes with L1b3). Each part's row has its layer (cut, score or engrave, L1c: `LaserData.ops`, only the parts not cut); overlaps are asked about only between two cut parts. Save SVG / Save DXF carry the layers. Styles: `src/styles/laser-page.css`.
- Saved data (`LaserData` in page.ts) has a version, `LASER_DATA_VERSION`, read when a project opens by the plugin's `readData` (`readLaserProjectData`): an older version is brought up to date a step at a time, a later one is refused as newer (core's `NewerFormatError`), so a build never stores again what it only half knows. A change to what the data holds takes the next number and a step there, with a test of both in `test/data-version.test.ts`. The page writes over what the project holds, so a field it has no name for stays.
- `src/flat-view.ts`: the flat view (L1a2): an SVG drawing over the viewport, the bed as an outlined sheet with a ruler (lines across it, numbers below and left), each part by the same path data the saved SVG file gets (`flatScene` in page.ts), the bed and the parts named, each part in its layer's look (`laser-flat-part-cut`, `-score`, `-engrave`) with a key to the layers in use above the bed's top-right corner. It only slides and zooms (drag, one finger, wheel, pinch, + − 0 and the arrows); its own row of buttons (Zoom out, Zoom in, Fit) in the 3D row's look, the 3D row hidden meanwhile (the view is put straight after the 3D canvas and the style sheet hides the `.view-controls` after it). Nothing is drawn before the page's first `show`; a fitted view fits again when its size or a card's (the parts list, a phone sheet) changes; hidden while the host says `data-drawing="off"` (the second pane's "Coming soon"). Parts are picked and moved here but kept by the page (`onPick`, `onMove`, `onTurn`): a click or tap picks, a mouse drags the part it presses, a finger only the picked one (so a slide across parts still slides the view), a second finger makes it a pinch; the dragged part's box lands on whole mm; arrows 1 mm (10 with Shift), R turns, Escape lets go; Turn joins the row of buttons while a part is picked. Styles: `src/styles/laser-flat.css`.
- `src/place.ts`: parts placed by hand, pure (L1b1): a `Placement` (angle, then where the box's lower-left corner sits), exact turns of an outline (corners and centres turn, bulges and radii keep their numbers; quarter turns on whole numbers), `layOutPlaced` (hand-placed parts where they were put, the rest in rows stepping round them; `layOut` exactly when nothing is placed), off-bed words, box overlaps and the pairs worth asking the kernel about (L1b2), snapping to whole mm.
- `src/overlap.ts`: whether two parts overlap, touch or lie apart (L1b2), every geometric answer the kernel's: boxes clear of each other settle it without a call; else the two outlines are tried as two cut-outs of one sheet (built: apart), and the regions the kernel makes of both parts' curves say whether any area is material of both. Where the kernel can't split edges lying along each other the second part is asked about again moved a ten-thousandth of a mm (the file's comment has the argument; to go when the kernel answers the question itself). "unknown" comes with why in words, and `meet` when the parts are known to be closer than the kernel's smallest feature. Never throws.
- `src/view-box.ts`: the flat view's camera, pure: the one drawing rule (bed mm to screen px), Fit inside margins and clear of cards lying over the view (the parts list), zoom about a point with limits, sliding with the bed kept in sight, the ruler's 1-2-5 steps, the wheel's rate (the 3D view's), whether a name fits.
- `src/sheet.ts`: the bed and the layout (rows from the lower-left corner, `gap` between parts and from the edges, a part that doesn't fit left off with why), exact moves of outlines, and the sheet's SVG (the page is the bed in mm, a group per layer, engrave first; each part's path data and box read from core's public `outlineToSvg` by `outlinePath`, so the arc maths stays in core until K1).
- `src/ops.ts`: layers (L1c), pure: cut, score, engrave, the beam's order (engrave, score, cut), each one's SVG paint and DXF layer and colour, reading and setting a part's layer, and which pairs' overlaps matter (two cut parts only).
- `src/dxf.ts`: the sheet as R12 DXF in mm: closed POLYLINEs with bulges (arcs exact), CIRCLEs, each part on its layer (ENGRAVE 7, SCORE 5, CUT 1), engrave first.
- Tests: `test/sheet.test.ts`, `test/dxf.test.ts`, `test/page.test.ts` (happy-dom), `test/flat-view.test.ts` (happy-dom), `test/view-box.test.ts` (every number worked by hand), `test/place.test.ts` (hand-worked turns, places and rows, two seeded), `test/overlap.test.ts` (the real kernel through packages/testing's node-kernel.ts: hand-worked pairs, boundaries at the kernel's own lines, seeded pairs against number models), `test/flag.test.ts`, `test/ops.test.ts`. A test never compares two elements with `assert.equal` (a failure prints the whole page and runs the test out of memory): compare `a === b` with `true`. Browser check: `apps/fission/e2e/laser-page.mjs` (in `npm run e2e`).
