# The Simulate workspace (TestLab)

Contents of `workspaces/simulate`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Its name for people is TestLab; its id stays `simulate` in code and in project files. The steps are in `docs/progress/testlab.md`; the solver and mesher are kernel work (the private kernel repo's `fea/` crate, from T3). Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- `src/index.ts`: the plugin. With `?testlab` (flag.ts) it mounts the page; without it, the Simulate placeholder it was. `readData` is `readTestLab`. The DRAFT `solve` still throws "not implemented": the page's Run is the way to solve (T5b).
- `src/flag.ts`: the `?testlab` switch (as Laser's `?laser`: kept for the tab, `?testlab=off` clears it), off until Ben releases TestLab.
- `src/study.ts`: TestLab's data (`TestLabData` version 3 in `project.workspaces.simulate` (version 3 adds a study's `joined`: `"all"` or the other bodies picked; `joinBodies`, `unjoin`, `withoutFaces`), version 2 read as not joined, version 1 read with no material: studies with a body by stable reference, a material by the library's id (`setMaterial`), holds and loads on faces by stable reference with the words they were picked by, mesh size; choices only, never results or geometry), `readTestLab` (each rule refused in words, a newer version as `NewerFormatError`), the pure edits (`addStudy`, `chooseBody`, `toggleHeld`, `togglePushed`: a face is held or pushed, never both; `addLoad`, `removeLoad`, `setLoad`: amount above 0 and at most `MAX_AMOUNT`, a pressure only square into its faces), `newtons` (kg × `G`), `loadWords`, `readyProblems`, `studyFaces`.
- `src/materials.ts` (T2): the materials a study can be made of (`testMaterials`, from core's `materialLibrary()`), `materialWords` (the numbers TestLab uses: the weaker way, across the layers, for a printed one), `sourceLines` (each number's source), `mpaWords`.
- `src/faces.ts`: a study looked up on the design now through core's registry (`facesOf("design", …)`, Design's face finder): `studyNow`, and `lostWords`/`studyLost` (a face an edit took away or gave to two faces, said in words on the hold or load that kept it, never dropped or moved to a guess).
- `src/page.ts`: the page (T1): Design's bodies listed through `objectsOf("design")` (the only one set up without asking), the study in the properties (Made of, T2: the material asked once with its numbers and sources; Held here, Pushed here with each load's amount, unit and direction, Add load, Run), faces picked by tapping the body (each tap one named undo step; picking puts a phone's Properties sheet away and ends on sleep), held faces and each load painted as overlays (`studyOverlays`). The study is a dependency-graph node (`studyNode`, expensive) read from every item its body is made from, so a design edit marks it out of date with a change note; the node stays through an unmount so an Undo's remount keeps the mark. Run (T5b) checks the set-up against the design now, borrows the body's kernel handle and faces from one rebuild (core's `withBody`, Design's lender) and calls the kernel's stress test in the background: the stage in words (`STAGE_WORDS`) with Cancel (`stopTesting`, `STOPPED`), every answer checked (`answerProblem`) and dropped if the set-up changed meanwhile; the result shown (`results.ts`) and in the view as a colour map (the shared viewport's `showColourMap`) with the result overlay; a design edit marks it out of date and the view shows the part as it is. T5c5a: while faces are picked a hint over the view (`#testlab-view-hint`, appended to the shared viewport's element, `pickHint`) says what a tap does. Styles: `src/styles/testlab-page.css`, `src/styles/testlab-results.css`, `src/styles/testlab-view-hint.css`.
- `src/join.ts` (TJ, Ben 2026-11-19): bodies joined for a test only, never in the design: `joinGroup` (the bodies a study joins as listed now, one no longer listed said in `missing`), `joinWords`, `listWords`, `joinedFaces` (the bodies' meshes and faces side by side, for picking before the union), `unionStudy` (the kernel's union history turned into each picked face's pieces on the joined part: refused when the bodies don't all touch, the history doesn't fit, or a face is held and pushed at once; a swallowed face lost in words, a merged one's notes), `withJoined` (lends each body, unions them with `body_boolean`, frees the result), `joinedNow`. The page's Join bodies… chooser, Change join and Unjoin; styles `src/styles/testlab-join.css`.
- `src/run.ts` (T5b): turning a study into the kernel's stress test and back: `faceFacts` (area, normal, flat within `FLAT_FACE`), `kernelLoads` (kg, N and kPa; a push "into" split per face by area), `stressTestOf` (MPa, the weaker way), `answerProblem` (the checks before anything is shown: `MAX_RESIDUAL`, `MAX_IMBALANCE`, and the safety factor measured against the biggest stress shown, the safe side), `drawnScale` (a bend over `MOST_DRAWN_BEND`, a tenth of the part's size, is drawn scaled down, below 1, and `scaleWords` says so: the small-bend maths stretched Ben's bar ten times its length, 19 Nov), `bentMesh`, the colour scale (`SCALE_COLOURS`, `scaleColour`, `vertexColours`), `safetyBand`, `safetyWords`, `ESTIMATE`, `figure`.
- `src/results.ts` (T5b): a checked answer as the panel and view show it: `resultOf`, `resultColours` (by stress or bend), `resultOverlays` (two dots, each its own legend line in its colour: `RESULT_OVERLAY` the red biggest stress, `BEND_OVERLAY` the violet biggest bend), `resultLines`, `factorWords`, `scaleWords`, `methodWords`, `resultSection`. T5c5c: the results card at the top of the section (`RunSetUp` copied at Run by `setUpOf`, `verdictWords`, `cardLines`, `pushedWords`, `limitWords`: where it would start to break, the loads times the safety factor; `summaryText` for Copy summary; `brokenWords`, said first in the details past breaking, when the card shows no stress or bend); the rest of the numbers behind How it was worked out.
- `src/suggest.ts` (T5c6): ways to make it hold more, read from a Run's own answer, under the results card (`suggestSection`, open when it would break or holds only just): thicker at the red dot (no number), a rounded inside corner only when the red dot is at one (`insideCornerNear`, `SHARP_TURN`; fillets not in Design yet, said so), holding it differently only when the red dot is where it is held (`heldNear`: a point exactly still), stronger materials (about SF × strength ratio: the stress here does not depend on stiffness), a printed part's layers (at most SF × along / across), and the load for a safety factor of `TARGET` (exact: linear). Styles: `src/styles/testlab-suggest.css`. Tests: `test/suggest.test.ts`; real kernel: packages/testing `journeys/testlab-suggest.journey.ts`; browser: `apps/fission/e2e/testlab-page.mjs` (the 1,000 kg run's suggestions at 1280 and 375 px).
- `src/test-file.ts` (T5c5d): Export test file, one run saved whole to send for checking (`TestFile`, format `spindlelabs-testlab-run` version 1; `ABOUT` explains every field inside the file): `RunRecord` (gathered by page.ts `runRecord` on the lent body before the test: the project as saved with only design and TestLab data, `keptProject`; the kernel's version; the part as STEP from `body_step`, or why not; every face, `fileFaces`; the test as sent), `testFileOf`, `testFileText`, `testFileName`, `readTestFile` (every field checked, the answer by `answerProblem`; newer as `NewerTestFileError`), `downloadTestFile`. The card's Export test file button (results.ts, `Result.record`). Re-run on the real kernel: packages/testing `src/testlab-rerun.ts`.
- Tests: `test/run.test.ts` (T5b, hand-worked: loads in N and MPa, PLA's numbers, every answer check broken and its 0.9×/1.1×, the drawn scale, bands, colours, the bent mesh), `test/study.test.ts` (hand-worked kg to N, the reader's every rule broken, 0.9×/1.1× of `MAX_AMOUNT`, version 1 read), `test/materials.test.ts` (each material's words by hand), `test/page.test.ts` (happy-dom over a stand-in Design), `test/flag.test.ts`, `test/join.test.ts` (TJ: the side-by-side faces and the union's history worked by hand, refusals), `test/results-card.test.ts` (T5c5c: the card by hand, Ben's 9,800 N run, Copy summary), `test/test-file.test.ts` (T5c5d: written and read back, faces by hand, every reader rule broken). The file run again on the real kernel: packages/testing `journeys/testlab-file.journey.ts`. On the real kernel with Design's own lister, face finder and graph sync: packages/testing `journeys/testlab-link.journey.ts`, and Run against beam theory: `journeys/testlab-run.journey.ts`, joined bodies: `journeys/testlab-join.journey.ts`. Browser checks: `apps/fission/e2e/testlab-page.mjs`, `apps/fission/e2e/testlab-join.mjs` (in `npm run e2e`).
